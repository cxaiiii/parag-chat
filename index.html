Meet Parag

Parag is a small chat model built by Chaitanya (cxaiiii), distilled from his own dataset. There is no server behind this page — the model itself downloads to your device and thinks right here in the browser.

Loads Parag v5 (0.8B) — one-time download of ~505 MB, cached for next time. Trained on multi-turn conversations, so it follows a thread instead of restarting every reply. Wi-Fi and a laptop give the best speed.
